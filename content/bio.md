I am a **Ph.D. candidate in Control Science and Engineering** at [Hangzhou Dianzi University](https://www.hdu.edu.cn/), supervised by Prof. Pingliang Zeng, and a **visiting Ph.D. student** at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/eee/), supervised by Prof. [Bendong Tan](https://tbendong.github.io/).

My research focuses on **uncertainty quantification and optimal operation of power systems**, with particular interests in probabilistic transient stability assessment and renewable energy integration. I study how uncertainty in renewable generation and loads affects system stability, and how it can be incorporated into planning and operational decisions.
