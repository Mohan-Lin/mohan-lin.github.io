Fundamental theory and technology of autonomically coordinated regional energy internet planning and operation based on generalized carbon emission trajectory.

- Investigated uncertainty-aware planning and operation of regional energy internet systems under carbon-neutrality targets.
- Conducted research on stochastic production simulation, energy-carbon coupling, and coordinated operation of shared energy storage.
