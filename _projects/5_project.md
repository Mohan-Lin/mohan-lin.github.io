Data-driven autonomous coordination for the optimal operation of large-scale distributed energy resource (DER) clusters in active distribution networks.

- Investigated data-driven aggregation and coordinated optimization of large-scale DER clusters.
- Developed surrogate and learning-based approaches for computationally efficient scheduling and real-time control.
