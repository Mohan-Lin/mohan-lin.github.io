Coordinated control technologies for county-level distribution grids with large-scale integration of energy-autonomous microgrids.

- Investigated topology identification, uncertainty-aware microgrid scheduling, and coordinated operation of distribution networks and microgrid clusters.
- Developed optimization and learning-based methods for improving operational economy and renewable energy accommodation.
